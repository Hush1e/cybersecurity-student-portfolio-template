# Instructions

These instructions walk you through setting up and maintaining your Cybersecurity Playbook. You do everything in a web browser. No software to install.

## Part 1: Set Up Your Playbook (Week 1 of CYBR-1100)

### Step 1. Create your GitHub account

1. Go to **github.com** and select **Sign up**.
2. Use a **personal email** you will keep after graduation.
3. Choose a **professional username**, such as `firstname-lastname` or `flastname-cyber`. Your username becomes your website address, so pick one you would put on a résumé.
4. After signing in, go to **Settings → Emails** and add your **@hocking.edu** email as a second address.

### Step 2. Copy the Playbook template

1. Open the template link your instructor provides.
2. Select the green **Use this template** button, then **Create a new repository**.
3. In **Repository name**, type your username followed by `.github.io` exactly. Example: `jsmith-cyber.github.io`
4. Select **Public**.
5. Select **Create repository**.

### Step 3. Turn on your website

1. In your new repository, select **Settings**.
2. In the left menu, select **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment → Branch**, choose **main** and **/(root)**, then select **Save**.
4. Wait 1–2 minutes. Your site will be live at `https://yourusername.github.io`.

### Step 4. Personalize your site

1. In your repository, select the file **_config.yml**, then the **pencil icon** (Edit).
2. Change only the three lines marked **CHANGE ME**. Keep the quotation marks.
3. Select **Commit changes**, then **Commit changes** again.
4. Open **index.md**, select the pencil icon, and replace every item in **[square brackets]** on your Home page.
5. To add your photo: open the **images** folder, select **Add file → Upload files**, upload your photo, and commit. Then change `avatar-placeholder.png` in index.md to your photo's file name.

### Step 5. Submit your link

Visit `https://yourusername.github.io` to confirm it works, then submit the link in Blackboard.

## Part 2: Rules for Your Playbook

- **Keep all eight knowledge-area pages.** Do not delete or rename them. Every course in the program adds to them.
- **Each artifact goes on one primary knowledge-area page.**
- **Every artifact follows the artifact format** in [Artifact Template](../artifact-template/).
- **Your commit history is part of your record.** It shows your growth across the program, so commit your work as you go.

## Part 3: Never Publish

Your Playbook is public on the internet. Never publish:

- Passwords, API keys, tokens, authentication or MFA codes, or private keys
- Private IP addresses, internal network details, or system configurations from real organizations
- Personally identifiable information (yours or anyone else's): phone numbers, addresses, student IDs, birthdates
- Confidential, proprietary, or employer data
- Unpatched vulnerability details for real systems

**Sanitize before you publish:** crop or blur screenshots, use fictional names and data, and remove anything from the list above.

## Part 4: Adding an Artifact

1. Open the knowledge-area page where the artifact belongs (for example `playbook/human-security.md`).
2. Select the pencil icon.
3. Copy the block from the [Artifact Template](../artifact-template/) and paste it under **Artifacts**.
4. Fill in every field. Upload any sanitized screenshots to the **images** folder first.
5. Commit your changes and check the live page.
